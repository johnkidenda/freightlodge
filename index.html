Why this exists

Freight LTL still runs on phone tag, email chains, and portals that were built for brokers, not for you. You need a price you can trust, a booking you can pay for, and a way to know where the pallet is. You do not need another system of record for your warehouse.

How it works

1

Tell us the move

Origin, destination, pallet count, weight, and any access notes. We come back with a door-to-door LTL quote you can read in one screen.

2

Book and pay

Accept the quote and pay by card. No chase for a credit application on the first move unless your account later needs one.

3

We run the lane

Pickup, linehaul, and delivery are handled. You get confirmation and tracking updates without sitting in a carrier portal.

What you get

Built for shippers, not for software teams

What this is not

Freight Lodge is a freight shop for door-to-door LTL. It is not a transportation management system, not a carrier portal, and not a rate API for you to rebuild your own stack.
